Greenfield University - Overview

Greenfield University is a private research university located in Greenfield City. It was founded in 1998 by the Greenfield Educational Trust. The university celebrated its silver jubilee in 2023.

The campus covers 120 acres and includes 42 academic buildings, 4 residential hostels, a central library, a sports complex, and a medical center. The motto of the university is "Knowledge for a Better Tomorrow".

Professor Sarah Mitchell has served as the Vice-Chancellor since 2019. The university currently enrolls approximately 12,000 students and employs 450 faculty members across six faculties.

The six faculties are: Faculty of Engineering and Technology, Faculty of Science, Faculty of Business Administration, Faculty of Arts and Humanities, Faculty of Medicine, and Faculty of Law.

Greenfield University is ranked among the top 50 universities in the national ranking published by the National Education Council in 2025. It holds accreditation from the National Accreditation Board with an A+ grade.

The university colors are green and gold, and the mascot is the Falcon. The official website is www.greenfield.edu and the main switchboard number is +1-555-0100.
