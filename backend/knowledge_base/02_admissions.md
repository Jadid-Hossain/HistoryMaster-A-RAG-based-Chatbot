# Admissions at Greenfield University

## Undergraduate Admission

Applicants for undergraduate programs must have completed higher secondary education (HSC or equivalent) with a minimum GPA of 3.50 out of 5.00. Admission is based on the university admission test (70 percent weight) and prior academic results (30 percent weight).

The application fee is 40 dollars for local students and 75 dollars for international students. Applications are submitted online through the student portal at apply.greenfield.edu.

## Important Deadlines

The application deadline for the Fall semester is June 15, and classes begin on September 1. The application deadline for the Spring semester is November 1, and classes begin on February 1. Late applications are not accepted.

## Graduate Admission

For master's programs, applicants need a bachelor's degree with at least a CGPA of 3.00 out of 4.00. PhD applicants must additionally pass a written test and an interview conducted by the respective department.

## International Students

International applicants must submit proof of English proficiency with a minimum TOEFL score of 80 or an IELTS band score of 6.0. The International Students Office in Room 105 of the Administration Building assists with visas and orientation.

## Contact

The Admissions Office can be reached at admissions@greenfield.edu or by phone at +1-555-0101. The office is located in the Administration Building, Room 102, and is open Sunday to Thursday from 9 AM to 4 PM.
