Sports, Clubs and Student Activities

Greenfield University encourages every student to join at least one club or sports team. Student activities are coordinated by the Office of Student Affairs in the Student Center building.

## Sports

The university has men's and women's teams for football, cricket, basketball, volleyball, and table tennis. The annual Inter-Department Sports Meet is held every March at the Central Sports Complex. The university football team, the Greenfield Falcons, won the National University Football Championship in 2024. The sports complex stays open from 6 AM to 9 PM and includes a swimming pool, a 400-meter running track, and four floodlit tennis courts.

## Major Clubs

The Robotics Club meets every Wednesday at 4 PM in the Robotics Lab of the Technology Building, and its faculty advisor is Dr. Nathan Brooks. The Robotics Club won the National Robotics Olympiad in 2023 and 2024.

The Debate Club meets every Thursday at 3 PM in Room 201 of the Arts Building under the guidance of faculty advisor Professor Laura Chen. The Programming Club organizes weekly contests every Friday evening and is advised by Dr. James Carter.

The Music Society rehearses in the Student Center on Tuesdays at 5 PM, the Photography Club organizes a campus photo walk every month, and the Drama Club stages two full-length plays every year in the university auditorium.

## Events

The biggest event of the year is the Spring Festival, a three-day cultural festival held in the first week of April with concerts, food stalls, and a gaming tournament. The Greenfield Hackathon, a 24-hour coding competition, takes place every October and attracts more than 500 participants from universities across the country.

Students can register for any club during Club Sign-Up Week at the start of each semester, or anytime by emailing the club's faculty advisor. A full list of the 27 registered clubs is available at the Office of Student Affairs, Room 112.
