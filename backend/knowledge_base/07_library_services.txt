Central Library Services

The Greenfield University Central Library is the academic heart of the campus. It is open from 8 AM to 10 PM on weekdays, from 9 AM to 8 PM on Saturdays, and remains closed on Fridays.

The library holds more than 200,000 printed books, 15,000 e-books, and subscriptions to the IEEE, ACM, Springer and JSTOR digital collections. Free access to all digital collections is available from any campus computer using a student ID login.

Students can borrow up to 5 books at a time, and each book can be kept for 14 days. A late fine of 25 cents per day is charged for each overdue book. Reference books, journals, and rare collections must be read inside the library and cannot be borrowed.

The library has two silent reading rooms with 300 seats in total on the second floor. Group discussion rooms can be booked at the front desk for up to 2 hours per day. Photocopy and scanning services are available on the ground floor at 5 cents per page.

Library cards are issued free of charge at the circulation desk in Room 101; students must bring their student ID and one passport photograph. Lost cards are replaced for a fee of 5 dollars.

The Digital Resources Help Desk on the first floor assists students with online journals, reference managers, and plagiarism-check tools. The library also runs a book reservation service: any borrowed book can be reserved online through the library portal at library.greenfield.edu.

Wi-Fi coverage extends to every floor of the library. The library hosts a "Research Skills Workshop" at the beginning of every semester, and the head librarian, Mrs. Gloria Sparks, can be reached at library@greenfield.edu or +1-555-0130.
