Campus News - Greenfield University

New AI Research Center Inaugurated

On 15 August 2026, Greenfield University inaugurated the Center for Artificial Intelligence Research (CAIR) on the third floor of the Technology Building. The center houses 24 GPU workstations and will host joint projects with industry partners. Dr. James Carter, head of the CSE department, has been appointed as the founding director of CAIR. The center will offer ten funded research assistantships to graduate students every year starting from Spring 2027.

Falcons Win the National Robotics Olympiad Again

The Greenfield University Robotics Club defended its title at the National Robotics Olympiad held on 2 September 2026, defeating 48 competing universities. The winning robot, named Falcon-X, completed the obstacle course in 94 seconds, the fastest time recorded in the competition's history. Team captain Meera Patel led the seven-member team advised by Dr. Nathan Brooks.

New Campus Bus Routes Announced

From 1 October 2026, the university transport office added two new bus routes: Route C connects the Central Library with the Railway Station every 30 minutes from 7 AM to 10 PM, and Route D serves the Lakeside residential area every hour. All full-time students ride every campus bus for free with a valid student ID card.

Greenfield Hackathon 2026 Registration Open

Registration for the Greenfield Hackathon 2026 is now open on the student portal. The 24-hour coding competition will be held on 17-18 October 2026 in the Software Engineering Lab and is expected to attract more than 500 participants. This year's theme is AI for Public Good, and the total prize pool is 10,000 dollars.
