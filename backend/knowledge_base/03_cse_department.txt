Department of Computer Science and Engineering (CSE)

The Department of Computer Science and Engineering is the largest department at Greenfield University. It currently has 45 faculty members, 6 staff members, and more than 800 students enrolled in its programs.

The head of the CSE department is Dr. James Carter, who has been leading the department since 2020. The department office is located in Room 210 of the Technology Building, and the office phone number is +1-555-0120.

Programs offered by the department include the Bachelor of Science in Computer Science and Engineering (4 years, 148 credit hours), the Master of Science in Computer Science (2 years, 36 credit hours), and the PhD in Computer Science (3 to 5 years).

The department operates six specialized laboratories. The Artificial Intelligence Lab is in Room 301, the Robotics Lab is in Room 305, the Networking Lab is in Room 310, the Software Engineering Lab is in Room 315, the Cybersecurity Lab is in Room 320, and the High Performance Computing Lab with 40 GPU workstations is in Room 325. All labs are on the third floor of the Technology Building and are open from 8 AM to 8 PM on weekdays.

Every year the department organizes the Greenfield Programming Carnival, the largest student programming contest on campus, usually held in January. The department also runs a student mentoring program where senior students help first-year students with programming fundamentals.

Graduates of the CSE department have an employment rate of 92 percent within six months of graduation, and the top recruiters include several national and multinational software companies.
