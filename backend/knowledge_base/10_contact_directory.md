## Emergency

The 24-hour campus emergency hotline is +1-555-0911. The campus medical center is located next to the sports complex and is open 24 hours; its emergency number is +1-555-0912. Security control room: +1-555-0913.

The IT Helpdesk resolves password resets, Wi-Fi problems, and portal access issues; walk-in service is available from 9 AM to 6 PM on working days, and tickets can be submitted anytime at helpdesk@greenfield.edu.
