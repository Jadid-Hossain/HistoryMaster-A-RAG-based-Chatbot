# Hostel and Accommodation Facilities

Greenfield University operates four residential hostels on campus. The two boys' hostels are called Ash Hall and Oak Hall, and the two girls' hostels are called Birch Hall and Cedar Hall. Together they can accommodate 1,800 students.

## Rooms and Amenities

Every hostel room is shared by two students and comes furnished with beds, study desks, wardrobes, and bookshelves. Each room has high-speed Wi-Fi, and hot water is available 24 hours a day. Common facilities include a gym, a TV room, a prayer room, laundry rooms with washing machines, and a small convenience store in each hostel.

## Mess and Dining

Each hostel has its own dining mess serving breakfast, lunch, and dinner. The monthly mess charge is included in the hostel fee. A standard menu is served every week, and students can request vegetarian meals during registration.

## Fees and Application

The hostel fee is 800 dollars per semester, which includes accommodation and meals from the mess. Students apply for hostel accommodation through the student portal at the beginning of each semester. Hostel seats are allotted on a first-come, first-served basis, so early application is recommended.

## Wardens and Rules

The warden of Ash Hall is Mr. Robert Hale, the warden of Oak Hall is Dr. Samuel Ortiz, the warden of Birch Hall is Mrs. Hannah Lee, and the warden of Cedar Hall is Dr. Amara Okafor. Each hostel is supported by 24-hour security guards and CCTV monitoring.

Visitors are allowed in the common rooms from 9 AM to 8 PM only. Entry gates close at 11 PM, and students returning later must register at the guard desk. Quiet hours are enforced from 10 PM to 6 AM during examination weeks.

For hostel complaints or maintenance requests, students should contact the Hostel Management Office in Room 5 of the Student Affairs Building or email hostel@greenfield.edu.
