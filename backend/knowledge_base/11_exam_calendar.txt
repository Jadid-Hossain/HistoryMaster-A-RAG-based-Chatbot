Academic Calendar and Examinations

The academic year at Greenfield University has two semesters: Fall (September to December) and Spring (February to June). Each semester has 14 teaching weeks followed by an examination period.

## Examination Schedule

Midterm examinations for the Fall 2026 semester will be held from October 20 to October 27. The final examinations for the Fall semester are scheduled from December 8 to December 19. For the Spring semester, midterms take place in the second week of April and finals run from June 1 to June 12.

The complete examination routine is published on the student portal at least four weeks before each examination period. Students must bring their student ID cards and admit cards to every examination.

## Results and Grading

Semester results are published within three weeks after the last examination on the student portal. The grading scale runs from A+ (90 percent and above, 4.00 grade points) down to F (below 40 percent, 0.00 grade points). A CGPA of at least 2.50 out of 4.00 is required to graduate.

## Retake and Improvement

Students who fail a course may take a retake examination in the following semester, and a student may retake a maximum of five courses during the entire program to improve grades. Retake registration closes two weeks after semester results are published.

## Other Important Dates

The last date for course registration and dropping courses without penalty is the 10th day of each semester. Tuition payment for each semester is due within the first two weeks; a late payment fee of 50 dollars applies after the deadline. The mid-semester break is one week long and follows the midterm examinations.
